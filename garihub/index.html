GariHub, by Amana

Every car, from the auction to the buyer's keys.

GariHub runs a Kenyan car import yard around one file per car: what it cost to land, where it is today, and what it sold for. Buying, shipping, clearing, the yard, sales and the books, in one place.

Talk to us See it at work

Built for the way a car yard works

Made for yards that bring in a hundred cars a month or more, mostly bought for stock.

Cars

One file per car

Each car has its own file, kept by chassis number: its stage, photos, documents and every cost from the auction to the handover.

Buying

Know your maximum bid

GariHub works out the most to bid in yen from what the car will sell for here, after freight, duty, clearing, repairs and your margin.

Buying

Exact landed cost

Buy in yen, pounds or dollars. Every freight, duty and clearing bill tagged to a car goes into that car's cost.

Shipping

Ships and arrivals

RoRo or container, when each ship reaches Mombasa, freight split per car, and a warning for cars that could miss the import age limit.

Clearing

KRA duty and clearing

A duty calculator from KRA's CRSP list, a clearing file per car with its document checklist, port free days and storage charges.

Yard

Check-in on a phone

Photos, checklist, mileage and bay at check-in; the bay map, repair jobs running late, keys out and stock take by QR label.

Sales

The deal jacket

A deposit reserves the car. Trade-ins, payment plans, bank finance, commissions and the printed sale agreement on one page.

Payments

M-Pesa and bank, matched

Pay Bill and Till payments come in matched to the right sale. Send a payment request to the buyer's phone.

Kenya

KRA eTIMS and payroll

Invoices sent to KRA eTIMS, and Kenyan payroll with PAYE, SHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy, P10 and P9A.

Customers

A tracking link per buyer

Each buyer gets a private page showing where their car is, with WhatsApp updates as it moves.

Approvals

Nothing slips through

Payments abroad, sales below the asking price, refunds and bids over the maximum wait for the owner's approval.

Accounts

Profit per car

Car profit, stock at landed cost checked against the books, debtors and creditors by age, and statements.
