How we work

Each organisation gets its own system, set up with you.

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us what you run, how big it is, and which banks and Pay Bills you use.

  2. Your own system

    Your own secure site, with your name and logo on the sign-in page.

  3. Bring in what you have

    Members, students, tenants or cars from Excel, and your opening balances from the old books.

  4. Your team starts work

    Each person signs in to what they do, on a computer or a phone.
